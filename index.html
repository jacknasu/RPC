📊 Bank Statement Input

Upload your bank statement Excel file (with Date, Debit, Credit, Balance columns).
Opening Balance row is picked up automatically — the balance already sitting in the account earns profit too. Any Debit (e.g. matured amount transferred out) reduces the account's real balance from that date onward, and profit from then on is calculated on the actual balance left in the account (taken straight from your statement's own Balance column) — not on the raw debit amount. Untick or remove any row below if you don't want it included.
⚠ Note: the balance remaining after a withdrawal (like the ₹2,000 left after a matured-amount transfer) is already included automatically when you click Calculate — you'll see it as its own Credit-style row there. Only add a manual entry below if you need to correct something the statement itself doesn't show — adding the same amount again here will double-count it.
